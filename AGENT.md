# Agent Notes — The Funniest Sick-Day Excuse

Entry: a single original one-line sick-day excuse (1–200 chars) written by
`main.py` (stdlib only) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

The line: a pulled muscle from reaching for the snooze button, with the body
"officially filing for a rest day" and the sender as just its messenger.
It is half believable, avoids the worn fever/dog/grandmother excuses, and
lands the joke near the end, in the voice of a real person texting their boss.
